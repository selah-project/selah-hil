# hil — Hiligaynon · NOTES

*Ini nga file nagarekord kon diin ang kamot ukon ang isa ka pagbuot
nagtandog sining badbad, kag kon ano ang nahibaluan namon nga wala pa
matadlong. Ang mga nota sa Ingles — ginasambit nila ang gramatika kag
ang mga ginhalinan nga Hebreo, kag ang Ingles amo ang lenguahe sang
buluhaton sa tanan nga pulungkoan.*

token, under the rails at
`docs/methodology/translation-discipline/hil.md` (selah repo).

## The Name

Hiligaynon Bibles print **GINOO** and **Dios**. This chair writes
**Yahweh**, and keeps the rest of the table: Elohim · Adonay · Shadday ·
El · Eloah · Yah · Tsevaot. Lowercase *dios* / *mga diyos* keeps its
lawful seat over the nations' gods, and *agalon* over a human lord.

**Name seats reading Yahweh: 6,828 of 6,828** — every token whose
Hebrew surface holds יהוה (counted 2026-09-24). The third gate read
6,555 of 6,555 at 22,332 verses. No erasure word stands on a Name seat.

## Hand-rendered verses: none

**No verse in this repository was written or spliced by hand.** No file
carries a `hand` field. Every verse is machine-pressed under the rails;
the repairs were re-presses, deletions for refill, and one surface
restore against the floor. Where a verse resisted, it was pressed
again, not written.

that provoked them:

- **Exodus 26:1 — the Mishkan called *ang pulungkoan***, the rails'

## Cruxes

- **Exodus 3:14** took the present: *Ako-Amo ang Ako-Amo*. Whether the
  seat is a Name or the verb is an open fork across the fleet.
- **Psalm 23:1** — מזמור came out **Dayoranon**, and רעי as
  *magabantay ⟨sang karnero⟩* (*keeper ⟨of the sheep⟩*). Both are for a
  native ear.
- **Ruth 3:9** — **goel** left transliterated, and אַתְּ (*ikaw*)
  correctly carries no marker: the consonants match את but the word is
  *you*.
- **Daniel 3:12** — the Aramaic object marker יתהון glossed `⟨ית⟩`, a
  marker the floor does not carry. Aramaic is unruled across the fleet.
- **אדון at a divine seat** — Isaiah 1:24, Joshua 3:11 and their like
  have no row of their own in the Name table; the chair wrote *Adonay*.
  **Genesis 23:6** is the mirror case: there אדני is the Hittites'
  address to Avraham — a human lord — and the chair wrote *Adonay*
  there too. The class is declared, not repaired.
- **Isaiah 53:5** landed *tungod sa iya mga labod* — *labod*, a wound,
  not the rails' word for a bracket. The fault that bit the Chichewa
  chair did not repeat here.

**The bleed probe, read back.** A whole-word scan for neighbour words
first fired on 53 verses for *asa*; read back, **52 of them are the king
Asa** and the last is the Hiligaynon verb *gin-asa*. *Karon* fired 34
times, and most of those are *yari karon* — lawful Hiligaynon for
*behold*. What survives the read-back is small: *kini* 15 · *ingon*
bare 17 · *ito* 8 · *dili* 5 · *ay* 5 · *iyon* 1. **Read the class back
before deleting anything** — that rule was learned on another chair and
it holds here.
